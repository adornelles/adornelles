### 01 / Research profile

I investigate problems shaped by **interdependence, uncertainty, and non-linear propagation**—from food systems and climate adaptation to environmental security, public health, and nature-related financial decisions.

My work combines **statistical modelling, causal inference, machine learning, and data visualisation** across large-scale, multi-layered, and longitudinal datasets. The problem shapes the toolkit, not the other way around: analytical choices should be driven by the question, available evidence, and decision context.

The aim is translational: to make complex relationships more **interpretable, attributable, and useful for action** without sacrificing methodological rigour.

> **Current focus** — AI-assisted analysis of genomic and clinical information, treatment outcomes, and therapeutic decision support in major depressive disorder.
